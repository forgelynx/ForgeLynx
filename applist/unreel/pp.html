Privacy Policy

Unreel: Know your scroll. Reclaim your time.

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction & Core Philosophy

At Lean Bytes, we believe your attention and digital habits belong to you alone. Unreel is a cross-platform application (Android and iOS) engineered to help you monitor, understand, and limit short-video scrolling (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and similar feeds).

Unreel is built on an offline-first, zero-knowledge architecture designed with strict privacy by design. We do not inspect your screen content, we do not require account sign-ups, and we never transmit your viewing habits to third-party ad networks or surveillance brokers.

2. Non-Negotiable Privacy Guarantees

1. Zero Content Inspection: Unreel never reads, parses, OCRs (optical character recognition), or stores on-screen text, captions, comments, creator usernames, video descriptions, or media streams.

2. 100% On-Device Processing: All detection signals, scroll event triggers, and dwell calculations are evaluated entirely on your physical hardware.

3. Local-Only Persistence: Session timestamps, reel counts, daily aggregates, and rule configurations are stored exclusively in your device's local database.

4. No Telemetry Leaks: Unreel does not send your browsing patterns or app usage history to external telemetry servers, trackers, or commercial advertising SDKs.

3. Information We Collect and Process

Unreel operates purely on minimal, anonymous metrics required to deliver its core counting and limit-enforcement features:

Data Point Purpose Storage Location
Reel Scroll Counts Quantify how many short videos you have swiped through during a session. Local on-device SQLite database only.
Session Timestamps & Dwell Calculate session length and active scroll time for daily analytics. Local on-device SQLite database only.
Target App Identifier Identify which supported app was active (e.g., Instagram, YouTube, TikTok). Local on-device SQLite database only.
User Preference Rules Store your custom reel limits, schedule windows, and nudge styles. Local Key-Value / Multiplatform Settings.

What We NEVER Collect or Store

  • No Text Content: We never inspect or store post titles, captions, hashtag descriptions, comments, or chat messages.
  • No Visual or Audio Data: Unreel never records your camera, microphone, screen video, or audio streams.
  • No Personal Identifiers: No names, phone numbers, email addresses, device serials, or advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA).
  • No Keystrokes: We never observe passwords, keystrokes, form fields, or search queries.

4. Platform-Specific Architecture & Data Handling

Android Implementation (Exact Reel Counting)

On Android devices, Unreel utilizes an AccessibilityService (ReelDetectorService) configured with the strict flag canRetrieveWindowContent="false".

  • The service monitors structural scroll events (e.g. typeViewScrolled and typeWindowStateChanged) specifically filtered to short-video containers within your selected target applications.
  • It detects page boundary transitions (new reels) without accessing or parsing the underlying textual or media contents of the view hierarchy.
  • When your daily scroll limit is reached, Unreel uses a local overlay (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) or system intent to display a gentle nudge or block screen.

iOS Implementation (Estimated Usage via Apple FamilyControls)

On Apple iOS devices, iOS strictly prohibits apps from inspecting or intercepting other applications' screens. Unreel interfaces natively with Apple's official FamilyControls and DeviceActivity frameworks.

  • Usage ticks are tracked via Apple system-level thresholds at 1-minute intervals under individual authorization.
  • Reel counts on iOS are marked as ESTIMATED and never mingled with exact counter logs.
  • All extensions (DeviceActivityMonitor, ShieldConfiguration, and WidgetKit) read purely from a secure local App Group container (group.app.unreel.shared) without remote communication.

5. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

To provide accurate detection and non-intrusive enforcement, Unreel requests the following explicit device permissions:

Android Permissions

  • BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE: Required to count scroll transitions in designated short-video apps. Strictly sandboxed with zero text/message reading capabilities.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Allows Unreel to display floating reel counters, live session indicators, and limit nudge dialogs over target apps.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS: Used to detect when a supported video application is in the foreground so the detector service only operates when relevant.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Keeps the detector and limit monitor alive in the background while you are scrolling reels to prevent system termination.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Delivers scheduled milestone summaries, nudge warnings, and limit alert banners.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Automatically reactivates your configured limits and scheduled protection windows after your phone restarts.

iOS Permissions

  • FamilyControls Authorization: System authorization granted by the user to allow Unreel to set Screen Time activity schedules and system shields.

6. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Unreel does not embed any third-party behavioral analytics, ad exchange SDKs, user fingerprinting libraries, or commercial data brokers.

Any crash diagnosis is limited to standard, anonymous operating system crash reporting provided directly by Google Play or Apple App Store if you have opted in at the OS level.

7. Data Retention & User Control

  • 100% User Ownership: Your scroll logs and session statistics remain exclusively in your local application database.
  • Reset Metrics: You can wipe today's metrics at any time using the "Reset Today" button in Settings.
  • Clear All Data: Uninstalling the application or selecting "Clear Data" in your device's system settings permanently erases all databases and preferences.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can grant or revoke any permission (Accessibility, Screen Time, Notifications, Draw Over Other Apps) at any time through your operating system settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Unreel does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. While Unreel can be used by families and students to establish healthy screen habits, no personal identity or surveillance information is ever gathered.
