2. Description of the Service

Unreel is a digital wellbeing and focus utility designed to help users track, understand, and regulate their consumption of short-form video content (such as Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok). Features include:

  • On-Device Reel Detection: Scroll event counting and dwell duration monitoring in supported video feeds.

  • Focus Metrics & Daily Trends: Local analytics to visualize scroll patterns and time reclaimed.
  • Glance & WidgetKit Integrations: Real-time home screen and lock screen widgets.

4. Platform Detection Differences & Accuracy Disclaimer

You acknowledge that reel detection mechanisms differ depending on operating system architecture:

  • Android: Counts are calculated from native AccessibilityNodeInfo scroll events (EXACT). However, because third-party platforms frequently update their user interfaces, temporary discrepancies in scroll detection may occur before app definition updates are applied.
  • iOS: Because Apple iOS prohibits direct observation of third-party interfaces, usage on iOS is derived via Apple's Screen Time FamilyControls and DeviceActivity frameworks (ESTIMATED). iOS counts represent time-threshold approximations and are clearly designated as such.

Unreel is an advisory self-management tool and does not guarantee complete prevention of unintended device usage.
